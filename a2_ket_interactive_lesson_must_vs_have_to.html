"We have to wear hiking shoes."

🔴 MUSTN'T

Dangerous / Forbidden:

"You mustn't feed wild monkeys."

BIG CLASSROOM NOTICE • CAMBRIDGE A2 KET

REMEMBER THE #1 DIFFERENCE!

🔴🚫

MUSTN'T

= DO NOT DO IT! (FORBIDDEN)

You have 0% Choice. It is against the rule!

"You mustn't use your phone in the exam."

🟢✨

DON'T HAVE TO

= NOT NECESSARY! (FREE CHOICE)

You have 100% Choice. You can do it if you want!

"You don't have to bring food. Lunch is free!"

🟡 And remember: HAVE TO / HAS TO / MUST = 100% Obligation (You have NO choice!)
